# GTM.md — Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Engineering managers and tech leads at startups (10–100 engineers) who manage AI tool budgets.
**Secondary:** Startup founders/CTOs who approve AI tool spend but don't track it closely.
**Tertiary:** Finance/ops teams at mid-stage companies doing annual vendor reviews.

## Value Proposition

"Find out if you're overspending on AI tools — in 60 seconds, for free."

SpendScan gives engineering leaders a defensible, shareable report they can use to justify budget changes to finance. The report is the product. Credex is the upsell.

## Distribution Channels

### 1. Organic / Content (Week 1–4)
- **Twitter/X thread:** "We analyzed pricing for 8 AI coding tools. Here's where most teams waste money." → link to SpendScan
- **LinkedIn post:** Target engineering managers with "Are you paying for Copilot AND Cursor? Here's why you might not need both."
- **Hacker News:** Show HN post — the audit engine source code is open and the logic is transparent
- **Dev.to / Hashnode:** "How I built a rule-based audit engine for AI tool spend in 7 days"

### 2. Viral Loop (Built-in)
- Every audit generates a shareable URL with OG preview cards
- Share text: "I could save $X/mo on AI tools" — engineered for social proof
- The shared page has a CTA: "Run your own audit →"

### 3. Community Seeding (Week 2–6)
- Post in relevant Slack communities: Rands Leadership, Engineering Managers, CTO Craft
- Reddit: r/ExperiencedDevs, r/StartupToolkit, r/SideProject
- IndieHackers: build-in-public log of the 7-day build

### 4. Credex Sales Integration (Ongoing)
- Credex sales team uses SpendScan as a discovery tool in outbound sequences
- "We ran your AI tool stack through our audit engine — here's what we found" (personalized cold email)
- High-savings audits (>$500/mo) trigger a warm Credex consultation CTA

## Messaging Framework

| Audience | Pain Point | Message |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Eng Manager | "I don't know if we're on the right plans" | "Get a per-tool breakdown with specific plan recommendations" |
| CTO/Founder | "AI tools are our 3rd biggest SaaS cost" | "Find $X/mo in savings — takes 60 seconds" |
| Finance | "I need data for the vendor review" | "Shareable audit report with defensible reasoning" |

## Conversion Funnel

1. **Land** → Landing page (SEO + social)
2. **Engage** → Fill out the audit form (2-3 minutes)
3. **Value** → See results with per-tool savings
4. **Capture** → Email gate (after value is shown)
5. **Share** → Shareable URL → viral loop
6. **Convert** → Credex CTA for >$500/mo savings teams

## Success Metrics (First 30 Days)

- 500+ completed audits
- 100+ email captures
- 50+ shared audit URLs
- 10+ Credex consultation bookings
- 3+ qualified leads from inbound
